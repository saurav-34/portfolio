COMBAT: Conditional World Models for Behavioral Agent Training
Oct 2025
Saurav Suman, with Wayfarer Labs · 1st Workshop on Reliable & Interactive World Models, ICCV 2025

A conditional world model that learns a reactive opponent's tactical policy as an emergent property, rather than encoding it explicitly.

AbstractHide abstract
We train a real-time diffusion model on the 1v1 fighting environment of Tekken 3, conditioning generation on player action history. The opponent's tactical behaviour is never supervised directly; it emerges from the model's need to predict future frames consistently. The resulting simulator produces an opponent that adapts dynamically to player actions, and runs at interactive rates. Co-authored in collaboration with Wayfarer Labs.

Arcade-JEPA — 1v1 JEPA vs humanOngoing

Four of the environments the world model is trained and evaluated on.

DetailsHide details

A JEPA world model, trained from scratch, that plays Atari 1v1 against a human from raw pixels. A CNN encoder and causal-transformer predictor (13M params) are trained self-supervised on 128×128 frames — no pretrained weights. The model is then frozen, and a Linear(192, 10) state head (~1.9K params) is the only thing trained on top, reading game state (ball/paddle positions and velocities) straight out of the latent with no access to emulator RAM. Stays accurate with up to 40% of the frame occluded.

One frozen-backbone recipe, six games. The Pong prototype was generalized into a multi-game arcade — Pong, Boxing, Tennis, Space War, Ice Hockey, Slime Volley (plus a Combat Tank config) — each with its own backbone trained the same way. Data collected through PettingZoo/ALE into Lance; every game wrapped in a game-agnostic Gym environment over the latent with exact train/deploy parity.

A small PPO policy that plays the game itself, instead of a hand-written rule. A ~117K-parameter policy head reads the same frozen latent and outputs actions directly, trained with PPO against a pool of opponents while the 13M backbone stays frozen. Built on sotoalt/lepong.

Checkpoints and a live in-browser demo — the model served over a WebSocket, playable in the page — to be released soon.

Edge-VLA — efficiency-centric vision-language-action models

QLoRA fine-tuning with 4-bit NF4 quantisation for edge robotics, cutting memory footprint roughly 4× while holding task accuracy.

DetailsHide details

Memory-efficient adaptation for edge robotics. Implemented a QLoRA fine-tuning pipeline with 4-bit NF4 quantisation and targeted LoRA adapters over a cached BF16 backbone, reducing the model memory footprint by roughly 4×.

Adapter and data-scale evaluation. Compared LoRA ranks 4, 8, 16 and 32 using 20, 50 and 100 demonstrations. Evaluated on ALOHA Sim Transfer Cube and LIBERO, reaching ~62 ms per inference step with no measurable accuracy degradation against the full-precision baseline.

Segmentation-guided pose estimation for retro environments

A Tekken 3 pose pipeline that uses SAM character masks and Kalman-filtered keypoint tracks to generate latent pose targets for world model training.

DetailsHide details

Constrained keypoint estimation. Low-resolution sprites and noisy backgrounds make direct pose estimation unreliable. Segment Anything Model (SAM) masks isolate each fighter and restrict keypoint localization to character pixels.

Temporal pose supervision. A Kalman filter tracks keypoints across frames to reduce jitter and preserve consistency during fast animations. The resulting pose trajectories provide latent ground-truth targets for a predictive world model.

Deep vision architectures from scratch

VGG-16/19, ResNet-50 and Vision Transformers implemented end to end in PyTorch, attention included. Comparative study of CNN inductive biases against ViT global receptive fields via gradient flow monitoring.

DetailsHide details

Architecture and training. Implemented the layers and attention mechanisms of VGG-16/19, ResNet-50 and Vision Transformers from scratch in PyTorch. Trained with SGD with momentum and AdamW, using cosine-annealed learning rates, batch normalisation and label smoothing.

Comparative analysis. Monitored gradient flow to compare CNN translation invariance with ViT global receptive fields, and evaluated the trained models using Top-1 and Top-5 accuracy.
